Samples with "SRFA" in the name are calibrants. They should only have a raw_data_identifier and match_confidence = "calibrant" in the output. They should not map to any existing biosamples.

"H2O" refers to water extracts. "Met" refers to methanol extracts. 
All files with extension ".d" are natural organic matter (NOM) data.
