Water extractable organic matter (WEOM) was obtained by shaking 6 g field moist soil with 30 mL MilliQ deionized water (1:5 w:v ratio) for 2 hours at 200 rpm, then centrifuging for 20 minutes at 4000 rpm. A portion of the supernatant was poured off for high resolution FTICR MS analysis. 

HCl-extractable organic matter (MAOM) was obtained by shaking 1 g of field moist soil with 40 mL 0.5M HCl (1:40 w:v ratio) at 150 rpm for 1 hour, then centrifuging for 20 minutes at 4000 rpm. A portion of the supernatant was poured off for FTICR MS.


Soil organic matter was characterized using Fourier-transform ion cyclotron resonance mass spectrometry (FTICR MS). This was done on the water-extractable organic matter as well as for HCl-extractable organic matter. WEOM and MAOM analyses were performed in triplicate, and all three replicates were analyzed via FTICR MS.

Sample preparation and analysis
The extracts prepared previously (water extracts and HCl extracts) were diluted to a standardized 2 mg/L C and acidified to pH 2 using concentrated HCl (37 %). The extracts were then desalted by solid phase extraction (Dittmar et al., 2008) using a Gilson GX-274 liquid handler equipped with Verity 4260 syringe pumps using Agilent Bond Elut PPL cartridges. 
Cartridge sorbent material was activated with 3 mL of Optima MeOH and conditioned with 5 mL of 10mM HCl. 15 mL of acidified sample was then loaded into cartridges and allowed to completely flow through. Cartridges were washed with a total volume of 50 mL of 10mM HCl (added in increments, maximum of 9 mL at a time) to remove interfering salts and minerals, while retaining organic compounds of interest. Cartridges were then dried with dry nitrogen gas at 20 psi over a 7.5 minute period to fully empty cartridge interior of remaining solvent. Samples were eluted using 1.5 mL of Optima MeOH and transferred into 2 mL Microsolv vials. Post-SPE desalted samples were kept at -80 °C with non-slit caps until ready for analysis. Once ready for analysis, non-slit caps were replaced with slit caps.

FTICR MS spectra were acquired in negative mode with electrospray ionisation (ESI) on a Bruker scimaX 7 Tesla FTICR mass spectrometer operating in quadrupolar detection mode, located at EMSL. Samples were infused at 4 μL/min using an automated flow injection system (Orton et al., 2018), with ESI high voltage of 3.3 kV, dry gas of 4 L/min at 200 °C. QC samples of MeOH (blanks) and Suwanee River Fulvic Acid (SRFA, obtained from IHSS) at 20 ppm in Optima MeOH were run at the start of, end of and regularly throughout each sample set to monitor instrument performance. Spectra for each sample were acquired in triplicate (FY23) or duplicate (FY24 onwards). Because there are 3 replicate extractions of each sample, each of which is measured in triplicate or duplicate, a total of 6 or 9 FTICR MS results are reported for each core section (top or bottom). For each acquisition, ions were accumulated for 10 ms prior to transfer to the ICR cell, where spectra were acquired between m/z 107 and 1100. 300 transients (2.09s, 8MW) were co-added for each acquisition, yielding a typical magnitude mode resolving power at m/z 400 of ~550k.
