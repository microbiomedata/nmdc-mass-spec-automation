HILICZ will be for polar metabolites.  POS and NEG go to same processed sample.
C18 will be associated with nonpolar metabolites. POS and NEG go to same processed sample.
If a biosample is mapped, it should have 4 associated raw files (HILICZ POS, HILICZ NEG, C18 POS, C18 NEG)

Files with "pilot" in name (like 20201023_JGI-AK_MK_506588_SoilWaterRep_pilot_QE-139_C18_USDAY59430_POS_MSMS_0_QC_Post_Rg80to1200-CE102040--QC_Run51.raw), these are from a pilot study. These should not match to any biosamples.

The "hydrophilic" and "hydrophobic" titles in the names of the biosamples (describing the soil) should not be confused with the polar/nonpolar aspects of the extractants.
